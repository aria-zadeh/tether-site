Privacy Policy

Last updated October 9, 2026

Tether collects no data. No one receives your name, tasks, schedule, device identifiers, location, or how you use the app. Tether makes no internet connections at all.

What stays on your device

Everything you enter: your first name if you give one, tasks and their steps, due dates, busy times, focus sessions, and settings. It is stored in Tether's private storage on your device and protected by your device's own security, such as your passcode. No one else has a copy or a way to see it.

The AI model

Step suggestions, easier rewrites of a step, and reading due dates from pasted text come from a small language model that is built into the app and runs on your device. Your text is processed on the device and never sent anywhere. The model does not learn from what you type. You can turn it off in Profile, Model, Tailored steps.

What Tether does not have

No accounts or sign-in. No server. No analytics, no ads, and no tracking. No third-party code that collects data. No crash reporter. No access to your location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on the device by Tether itself. No push service is involved. A reminder may show a task's title, including on the lock screen. You can change how notifications appear in the iOS Settings app.

Export and import

Export my data writes a backup file on your device and opens the iOS share sheet. Where you send it is your choice; Tether never uploads it. Import data reads only the file you pick.

Apple

Apple runs the App Store and handles your download under its own privacy policy. If you have chosen in iOS settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may share anonymous crash reports and usage statistics with Tether's developer. These do not identify you and contain none of your tasks.

Children

Tether is meant for high school students. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children under 13. Parents can see everything Tether stores by opening the app, and can delete all of it.

Deleting your data

Profile, Delete all data removes everything Tether has stored. Deleting the app does the same. There is nothing anywhere else to delete.

Your rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR and the California Consumer Privacy Act give you rights over personal information a company holds about you. Tether holds none, so there is nothing to access, correct, sell, share, or delete. Tether does not sell or share personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on the support page and in the app with a new date. If Tether ever starts collecting any data, this policy and the App Store privacy label will change first.

About the AI

Last updated October 9, 2026

Tether includes a small AI language model, a fine-tuned version of the open SmolLM2-360M-Instruct model (Apache License 2.0). It is built into the app, runs only on your iPhone, and never connects to the internet.

What it does

  • Suggests three to seven small steps for a task.
  • Rewrites one step to be easier.
  • Reads assignments and due dates out of text you paste.

What to know

  • It can be wrong. Steps can be generic, miss part of the assignment, or not fit what your teacher asked for. Dates read from pasted text can be off.
  • Nobody reviews its suggestions before you see them.
  • Tether checks every suggestion for offensive language and replaces anything that fails with general steps. No check catches everything.
  • Your teacher's instructions always come first. Check every due date yourself.
  • Judge every step yourself, including whether it is safe. Use your own best judgment and do not just follow the steps.
  • It breaks work into first steps. It does not write or answer your assignment. Follow your school's rules about AI tools.

Your data

What you type is processed on your device and never sent anywhere. The model does not learn from you.

Turning it off

Profile, Model, Tailored steps. With it off, Tether uses general steps written in advance instead.

Report a problem

If a suggestion is inappropriate or harmful, report it with what you typed and what it showed. Email tether.team.support@gmail.com.

Open source

Tether's on-device model is a fine-tuned version of SmolLM2-360M-Instruct (Apache License 2.0). The Instrument Sans typeface is used under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Full license texts are in the app under Profile, Licenses.

Privacy policy

Tether collects no data. None. Not your name, your tasks, your schedule, your device identifiers, or how you use the app.

  • Tether makes no network requests. It has no server, no account system, no analytics, no crash reporting, and no advertising.
  • Everything you type is stored only on your device, in the app's own storage.
  • Task breakdowns and extraction are produced by a language model that ships inside the app and runs entirely on your device. Your text never leaves the phone.
  • Notifications are scheduled locally by the app. No push notification service is used.
  • "Export my data" creates a file on your device and hands it to the system share sheet. Where you send it is your choice; Tether never uploads it.
  • Deleting the app, or tapping "Delete all data" in Profile, removes everything. There is nothing anywhere else to delete.

Tether is a productivity tool. It does not diagnose, treat, or manage any condition.

Last updated September 19, 2026. If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
